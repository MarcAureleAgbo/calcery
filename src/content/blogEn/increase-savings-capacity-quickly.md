Savings capacity is the surplus left after real costs, including irregular ones—not a number that works only in a perfect month.

Someone with €90 left each month cuts €35 of fees and subscriptions and €60 of recurring flexible spending, then sets aside €25 for annual costs they had missed. The new surplus is €160, not €185. Automating €120 leaves a €40 buffer. Keep that amount for three months; if it must repeatedly be cancelled, reduce it and fix the cause before increasing it.

Use the [monthly savings capacity calculator](/en/finance/monthly-savings-capacity/) for the baseline and the [savings goal timeline calculator](/en/finance/savings-goal-timeline/) to see how long a sustainable contribution may take to reach a goal.
