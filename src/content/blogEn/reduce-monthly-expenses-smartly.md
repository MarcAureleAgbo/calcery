Smart expense reduction starts with large, recurring, or no-longer-useful costs—not with punishing every small comfort.

## Match the action to the expense

Fixed costs may need a comparison or negotiation; variable costs may need a cap or substitute. Put subscriptions and recurring fees on their own line so small leaks cannot hide.

Someone looking for €140 ends €18 of unused subscriptions, saves €24 on insurance, replaces deliveries for €55, and limits impulse purchases by €45. The realistic gain is €142, without eliminating all leisure. Track the expected and actual gain for two months, then direct what remains to a priority rather than letting it disappear into daily spending.

Use the [monthly budget calculator](/en/finance/monthly-budget/) to check the new margin. If one fixed cost is structurally too high, a long list of tips will not solve it.
