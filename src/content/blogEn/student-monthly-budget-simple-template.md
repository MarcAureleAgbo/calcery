A student budget has to handle funding that changes and costs that arrive in a lump. Separate grants, confirmed family help, and student-job income before deciding what can be spent.

## A practical month

With €300 grant income, €200 family help, and a job that ranges from €180 to €320, use €680 as the base rather than assuming €800. A shared-flat budget might include €340 rent and utilities, €145 food, €35 transport, €30 phone and supplies, €45 social spending, and €35 for surprises, leaving €50. Put another €25 aside each month for books, enrollment, or a replacement laptop.

The money left over is for day-to-day life and surprises, not an annual cost you forgot. Revisit the plan after a housing, timetable, or funding change. Start with the [monthly budget calculator](/en/finance/monthly-budget/), then use your own actual figures.
