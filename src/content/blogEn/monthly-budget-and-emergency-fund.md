A budget makes an emergency fund fundable month after month. This page is about the saving pace; [the emergency fund guide](/en/blog/emergency-fund-guide/) helps set the target size.

With a €2,400 target and €120 available each month, the path is roughly twenty months without return assumptions. In a tight month, saving €50 rather than zero preserves the habit; a stronger month can make up some ground. Automate a contribution after essential costs and annual provisions, and keep the fund separate but quickly accessible.

When money is tight, protect housing, food, and due payments before secondary goals. Review only the amount reached, the transfer made, and any event that changed the plan. The [monthly savings capacity calculator](/en/finance/monthly-savings-capacity/) can help select the starting amount.
