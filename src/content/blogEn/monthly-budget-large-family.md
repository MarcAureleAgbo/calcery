Large-family budgeting is not a single-person plan multiplied several times. Housing and food are shared; school, clothing, and activities change child by child.

## Use separate pots for shared and individual costs

Keep food, housing, shared transport, and family leisure separate from clothing, supplies, activities, and routine health costs. Spread seasonal costs—school start, camps, winter clothes, holidays—across the year.

## Example for a five-person household

On €4,100 income, a household budgets €1,350 housing, €900 food, €310 transport, €210 insurance and health, €260 clothing and school, €170 leisure, and €220 annual-cost provisions. That leaves €680 for savings and adjustments. Saving €75 monthly for a €900 school-start cost avoids taking it from groceries in September.

The [complete monthly budget guide](/en/blog/complete-monthly-budget-guide/) covers the common base; this page is about seeing family costs before they arrive.
