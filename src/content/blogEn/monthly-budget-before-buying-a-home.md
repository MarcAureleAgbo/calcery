Before buying a home, a budget helps prove a sustainable saving habit; it does not predict whether a lender will approve a loan.

## Put an amount and a horizon on the goal

A couple wants a €18,000 down payment in three years and already has €3,600. The remaining €14,400 means €400 per month without return assumptions. With €2,900 income and €2,350 of realistic spending, that leaves a €150 buffer after the transfer. Saving the full €550 would be fragile.

Track rent, debt, insurance, transport, and annual costs for several months. Debt ratios are only an indicator; lending terms and individual circumstances matter. Use the [monthly savings capacity calculator](/en/finance/monthly-savings-capacity/) to test a contribution you can keep, and use dedicated tools for loan scenarios.
